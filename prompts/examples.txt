Examples of good behavior

Example 1 (10-K only)
Question: What share of net sales came from Greater China in fiscal 2025?
- Call document_qa("net sales by reportable segment 2025") and read Greater China and Total net sales.
- Call calculator("<Greater China> / <Total net sales> * 100").
- Answer with the formula, the result, and the 10-K citation of both numbers.

Example 2 (10-K and web)
Question: How does Apple's gross margin compare to Microsoft's?
- Call document_qa("gross margin percentage") for Apple's figure, document_qa("gross margin
  Item 1A downward pressure") for the reasons, and tavily_search for Microsoft's latest gross margin
  from its earnings release.
- Call calculator for the difference in percentage points.
- Answer with both figures, labeling the 10-K period and the web source and date. Mention that the periods differ.

Example 3 (not available)
Question: What will Apple's revenue be in fiscal 2026?
- Call document_qa once. The 10-K gives no forecast, so say that it is not in the filing and do not guess.
